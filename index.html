SIRDILUM ALUMINUM FITTINGS NIG ENTERPRISE is a legally incorporated Nigerian enterprise duly registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), Federal Republic of Nigeria (CRBN: 716623).

Answers & Enterprise Policies

Frequently Asked Questions & Service Policies

Transparent policies regarding certified aluminum gauges, CAC legal registration, custom workshop fabrication, warranties, and site installation.

Material Thickness Policy

We maintain an uncompromising material gauge policy. We exclusively fabricate with certified architectural 6063-T5 aluminum extrusions ranging from 1.2mm to 1.6mm wall thickness for casement windows, sliding doors, and shopfronts. For aluminum roofing (Steptile and Longspan), we guarantee genuine 0.45mm to 0.70mm metal gauges. Every raw material bundle is physically verified with digital thickness calipers before fabrication begins.

Legal Enterprise Accreditation

Yes. SIRDILUM ALUMINUM FITTINGS NIG ENTERPRISE is legally incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act by the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), Federal Republic of Nigeria, with Certificate Registration Number CRBN 716623. All our commercial invoices, bill of quantities (BOQ), and fabrication contracts are legally binding.

Quality Guarantee Policy

We stand behind our fabrication with a 5-Year Structural Integrity Warranty covering frame corners, miters, roller mechanisms, and lock alignments. We also offer a 10-Year Anti-Fade & Anti-Perforation Warranty on our electrostatically powder-coated profiles and roofing finishes. If any fabrication defect or seal leak occurs under standard usage, our technicians resolve it at zero cost.

Commercial Payment Terms

Our payment policy is designed for client peace of mind: 70% mobilization deposit upon contract signing and final site laser measurement to procure extrusions and hardware; 20% progress payment upon delivery of fabricated units to the site; and the remaining 10% balance upon completion of rigging, installation, and client sign-off.

Turnkey Service Delivery

Once an initial estimate is agreed upon, our chief engineer visits your building site in Lagos or other states to take millimeter-precise laser measurements. Standard workshop fabrication takes 5 to 10 working days depending on scope. Our on-site crew then executes professional anchoring, laser-guided plumbing, thermal gap setting, and waterproof EPDM caulking.

Core Enterprise Philosophy

Our official motto defines every product we manufacture: SAFE means shatter-resistant tempered safety glass and anti-burglar multi-point locking deadbolts. STRONG means guaranteed genuine metal gauges that withstand heavy coastal winds and corrosion. HIGH STANDARD, GOOD COMPROMISE means delivering bespoke architectural elegance while accommodating client project budgets through cost-efficient engineering without cutting corners.
